Section 5 • Unified Reporting Recommendation

Definitive operational recommendation on what the client should report and why

Executive Recommendation: Dual-Metric Governance Framework

The client should not force a false choice between the 58% and 71% figures. Instead, establish clear separation between retrospective financial/payer reporting and operational case management.

1. Primary Headline Metric: Standardize on Pharmacy Claims PDC ≥ 80%

PDC is the universally recognized gold standard accepted by CMS, NCQA, FDA, and commercial payers. It is objective, mathematically verifiable, and immune to patient recall bias or case manager recording inconsistency. All external performance guarantees, P&T dossier submissions, and brand leadership KPIs should report PDC ≥ 80% as the official headline adherence rate.

2. Complementary Operational Metric: Reposition CRM Refill Flag as an Early Triage Tool

The 71% CRM Refill-on-Schedule figure should NOT be reported as medication adherence. It should be explicitly rebranded as "PSP Refill Touchpoint Concordance". It serves as an operational leading indicator for case managers to identify emerging refill delays and trigger proactive barrier assistance before cumulative PDC drops below 80%.

3. Standardized Harmonization Rules

Standardize denominators by defining a common eligibility cohort: require active enrollment and at least 1 adjudicated pharmacy dispense. Require CRM outreach to query retail fills and verify clinical sample usage to eliminate data leakage.
